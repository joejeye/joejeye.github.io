ABOUT

Hi, I’m Yuxiao Zhai 👋

I’m a software developer based in the Greater Toronto Area with a background in wireless communications research. I hold a Master of Applied Science from the University of Toronto (Electrical & Computer Engineering) and a Bachelor of Engineering from Beihang University.

What I Do

I build software across the full stack — from backend systems in Java and Go to frontends in React and Vue.js. I’ve designed blockchain applications, deployed LLMs, built monitoring pipelines with Prometheus and Grafana, and written CLI tools in Rust. My current work involves large-scale telematics and fleet data systems.

Where I Came From

My research background is in 5G wireless networks — specifically interference mitigation in integrated access and backhaul (IAB) networks. I spent years deep in 3GPP specifications and MATLAB simulations. Before that, I worked on signal processing for astronomical observation at the Chinese Academy of Sciences, where I co-authored a patent on TDOA-based positioning.

What Drives Me

I have a long-standing love for mathematics — linear algebra, differential geometry, Fourier analysis — and I enjoy deriving things from first principles rather than reaching for a library. Some of my posts here reflect that: building models to understand the world, one coordinate system at a time.
